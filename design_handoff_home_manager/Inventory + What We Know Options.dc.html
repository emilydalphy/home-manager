Why this one
Nine sections become nine rows, each with the answer already visible in the summary line — so most of the time you read and leave without opening anything. Sections with a gap wear a gold edge, which is how the completeness card stops being decorative.
Trade-off: editing is two taps deep, and only one section is expanded at a time. If you edit these often, 5d is better.
