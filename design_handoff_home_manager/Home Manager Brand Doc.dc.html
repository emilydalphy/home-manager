Voice & tone

Helpful, reliable, warm, friendly, happy. Talk like a capable housemate, not a productivity app — plain, encouraging, never bossy or clinical.

Do
"Nice, laundry's done! What's next?"
"Chicken's thawing — you're set for dinner."
Don't
"Task completed successfully."
"Error: item not thawed within expected timeframe."
