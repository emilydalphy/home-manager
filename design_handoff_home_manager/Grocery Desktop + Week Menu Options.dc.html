My read
6a is the one I'd build. It's the only layout where all 21 meals are on screen at once with nothing to scroll, and it keeps the day card as home — so the everyday view stays calm and the full week is one pull-up away. The trade is small type in the grid; it reads fine for scanning, and any cell taps through to the day.
6b is the most readable and the least new interaction — but seven full-size day cards means about two screens of scroll, so "the whole week at a glance" is only half true.
6c is the most distinctive and the best for planning one slot at a time, but no single view shows a whole day together, and sideways scrolling hides days off-screen.
Grocery desktop · 5e vs 5f
5f. It keeps the mobile list shape in the middle, so both formats stay one design; the left rail replaces mobile's filter menu and the right panel earns the extra width with trip context. 5e's real idea is drag-to-assign — that survives inside 5f as a pinned "Unassigned" group at the top of the list, so you don't lose it by picking 5f.
Turn 5 · pick and choose
Grocery on desktop, and a week that reads like a menu
Grocery's mobile list stays exactly as it is — these three only change what happens above roughly 900px, where a 720px column strands the whole right half of the screen. The three week layouts borrow the share view's paper, rotated slot badges and dinner star, at three different volumes.

How to choose
5h is the safest — it's the share card you already shipped, so the app and the link people get look like the same restaurant.
5i is the best week-at-a-glance and the most menu-like on desktop; the seven columns are also the natural place to fix a gap without leaving the page.
5j has the most personality but pushes the rest of the week down; it suits a household that mostly wants to know about tonight.
These mix — see 5k, which is 5h's day card with 5j's week always in view.
5k
Week · The table card, with the week alongside
The merge you asked for: 5h's day card — violet day rail, rotated slot badges, dinner starred — with 5j's full-week list permanently in view, so a day's detail and the whole week never trade places.
