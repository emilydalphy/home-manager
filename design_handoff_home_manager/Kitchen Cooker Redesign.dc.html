Kitchen + Cooker, merged
One screen for cooking, not a list of everything
The live Cooker view dumps progress bars, the whole prep schedule and every meal of the week into one scroll, so the thing you're doing right now is buried. This keeps all of it but sorts it: tonight's cook is the screen, the week is a second view, and inventory and what-we-know stay where they already live in Kitchen.
Walk the steps — mobile shows one at a time, desktop shows all six with the current one lit.
Change servings; quantities rescale and "to taste" gets flagged instead of guessed.
Check ingredients and do-ahead prep, then finish the last step to close the night out.
Tap hands-free, or switch to The week to check meals off.
