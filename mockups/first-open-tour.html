First-open tour

Six stops, starting on the household's first draft ("Here's your week.") and ending back on it. Each stop dims the real screen, rings one real control in apricot, and puts a card beside it. Next moves on; Skip the tour (or Esc) ends it for good. Done or skipped is saved for the person (the household when nobody's picked), on the server.

A stop whose control isn't on screen this week (no open meal to Pick, no list yet) still shows its card, centred, with no ring. Phones are 390px; switch your system to dark to see the dark tokens.
