Getting help

In Pomona, open Preferences (the gear at the top of the main screens) and choose Something not working? Say what happened in your own words. It goes straight to the person who looks after Pomona, along with which screen you were on.

Can’t get into the app? Email [support email] and we’ll get back to you.

Deleting your household

Open Preferences and choose Delete your household at the very bottom. Type DELETE to confirm. Everything in your household goes straight away, and there’s no undo. Daily backups keep a copy for up to __BACKUP_GONE_WITHIN_DAYS__ days, then that’s gone too.

If someone else in your household still wants Pomona, you can leave instead. Your own details go, and theirs stay.

Questions people ask

Does Pomona share my household’s details?

Only with Anthropic, whose AI (Claude) does the planning and chat, and only after someone in your household taps Allow. You can turn it off in Preferences, under Sharing with Claude. No ads and no trackers. The privacy policy has the whole list.

Can I trust the allergy check?

Treat it as a helping hand, not a guarantee. Pomona tries to keep the allergens you’ve told it about out of your plans, but it can miss one. Always check labels yourself.

How do I add someone else in my household?

Open Preferences, then Who’s here, and add them. For another adult, tap Invite: Pomona makes a one-time link they open on their own phone.

I forgot our passphrase.

Ask whoever set up your household, or email [support email].

How do I stop the morning text?

Preferences, then Morning text. Turn it off there.
