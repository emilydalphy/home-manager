Skewers (Kafta Kebabs, Lamb Souvlaki, Moo Ping, Pinchos Morunos, Chicken Yakitori): `skewers` has a fixed golden meat colour, so lamb and beef can't be brown and the five plates look alike. Needs a meat colour value. Kafta Kebabs uses `rolls` for now.
Tsukune: needs a skewer of round glazed meatballs; nuggets show no skewer.
Steamed Pork Buns: needs a round white bao with pleats on top; plain rounds used for now.
Spiced Candied Pecans: needs a pecan half shape with a centre groove; nuggets read as brown lumps.
Bamia: needs a whole baby okra pod; strips stand in.
Burgers (Black Bean Burgers, Smash Burgers): built side-on from block and cutlet; a `burger` part would keep them consistent.
