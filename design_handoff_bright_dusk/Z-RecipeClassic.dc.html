Aloo Matar

Serves 4 · 50 min
Ingredients
Yukon Gold potatoes1.5 lbs
Frozen peas1 cup
Yellow onion1
Roma tomatoes3
Ginger1 tsp grated
Garlic2 cloves
Green chili1
Cilantro2 tbsp chopped
Vegetable oil2 tbsp
Cumin seeds3/4 tsp
Cinnamon stick1/2 inch piece
Green cardamom pods2
Kasuri methi1 tsp
Ground turmeric1/2 tsp
Kashmiri chili powder1 tsp
Ground coriander1 tsp
Garam masala1/2 tsp
Salt1 1/4 tsp
Steps
1Peel the potatoes and cut them into 1 inch cubes. Keep them in a bowl of cold water until you need them.
2Finely chop the onion and the 3 tomatoes. Grate 1 tsp of the ginger and mince the 2 garlic cloves.
3Slice the green chili thinly and put it in a small bowl for the table.
4Heat the 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pot with a lid over medium heat. Add the 3/4 tsp cumin seeds, the cinnamon and the 2 cardamom pods, and crush the 1 tsp kasuri methi between your palms into the pot. After about 30 seconds the cumin will sizzle and smell toasty.
5Turn the heat to low and stir in the 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chili powder and 1 tsp ground coriander for 30 seconds. Don't let them darken.
6Add the onion with 1/4 tsp of the salt and turn the heat back to medium. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring now and then, until it's soft and golden. If the spices start to stick, add a splash of water.
7Stir in the ginger and garlic and cook for about a minute, until they stop smelling raw.
8Add the tomatoes and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, mashing them with the spoon, until they're soft and mushy and you can see oil at the edges.
9Drain the potatoes and add them to the pot with the remaining 1 tsp salt. Stir for 2 minutes, so every piece is coated in the masala.
10Add 1 cup of the frozen peas and 1 1/2 cups water. Bring it to a boil, then cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring a couple of times. The potatoes are done when a knife slides through a cube with no push.
11Mash a few potato cubes against the side of the pot to thicken the gravy. If it's thicker than you like, stir in a splash of water.
12Stir in the 1/2 tsp garam masala and 2 tbsp chopped cilantro. Taste it. If it's flat, add a pinch more salt.
13Serve with roti or rice, with the sliced green chili on the side.
