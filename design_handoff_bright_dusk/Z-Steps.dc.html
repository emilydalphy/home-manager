Pork Tacos

Takes about 35 min
1Heat the oven to 325°F with a rack in the lower third. Pat the 2 lbs pork dry and cut it into 4 big chunks. Rub them all over with 1 1/4 tsp of the salt and the 1/2 tsp pepper.
2Stir the 1 1/2 tsp oregano, 1 tsp of the cumin and 1 tbsp of the oil together in a small bowl and rub it over the pork.
