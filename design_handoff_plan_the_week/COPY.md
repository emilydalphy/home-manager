## Question 1 — nights

| Element | String |
| --- | --- |
| Title | \`Which nights should I work around?\` |
| Why | \`Why I'm asking: this is what stops me putting a 50-minute braise on the night you're at tee-ball. Every night you tag, I plan differently — and I'll tell you how.\` |
| Tags | \`Regular night\` · \`Nobody home\` · \`Short on time\` · \`Hosting guests\` · \`Leftovers\` |
| CTA (tagged) | \`Next — {n} nights noted\` |
| CTA (none) | \`Next — every night is a regular one\` |

### Acknowledgements ("I'll …")

| Tag | String |
| --- | --- |
| Regular night | \`plan a normal dinner you cook that evening.\` |
| Nobody home | \`plan nothing and buy nothing for this night.\` |
| Short on time | \`keep it under 20 minutes, or make the night before stretch to cover it.\` |
| Hosting guests | \`scale the recipe and the shopping to the bigger table.\` |
| Leftovers | \`cook enough the night before instead of planning something new.\` |

Multiple tags join with \`And I'll \`.
