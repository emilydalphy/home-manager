Turn 3
Flow fixes — read from the live app
What's breaking the flow right now
1 · Navigation is a sentence
Six destinations live as 0.85rem text links inside a subtitle paragraph. No hierarchy, no current-page marker, and on a phone it wraps into an unreadable ribbon.
2 · Home shows no state
The landing screen is an empty chat box. To learn what's for dinner you have to type a question — the app knows the answer and won't say it unprompted.
3 · Setup and daily use are mixed
"Set up chores" sits next to "This week's chores" in one flat row of seven equal buttons. Day 1 and day 30 compete for the same space forever.
4 · Every page is a dead end
Grocery, Cooker, Inventory and Memory are separate full-page loads with no persistent nav and no route back except the browser — and the assistant isn't reachable from any of them.
5 · No "today"
The most common need — what am I cooking, what do I owe the house — has no home. It's split across a chat reply, /cooker and /grocery.
6 · Two doors to the same facts
"Edit household setup" and "What we know" both edit household truth. Users can't tell which one to trust.
All three options share the same four fixes: persistent nav (bottom tabs on phone, rail on laptop), a surface that answers before you ask, the assistant reachable from every screen, and setup folded into What we know. They differ in where the assistant sits in the flow.
