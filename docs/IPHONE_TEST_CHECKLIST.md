# iPhone test checklist

For Emily and Vineeth, on the first TestFlight build, before it goes to
Apple's beta review. About 30 minutes each. Use your own iPhone.

Make a fresh household for this, with an email address that isn't already
on Pomona. You'll delete it at the end.

Tick each one. If one doesn't work, write down what you saw, take a
screenshot, and keep going.

## Getting in

- [ ] Pomona opens from the TestFlight app and shows the sign-in screen.
- [ ] Sign up with your email. The 6-digit code arrives, and typing it gets
      you in.
- [ ] Pomona asks before sharing anything with Claude. "Not now" works, and
      so does saying yes.
- [ ] Swipe Pomona away completely, open it again: you're still signed in.
- [ ] Turn the phone off and on, open Pomona: still signed in.

## The week

- [ ] Plan a week: answer the questions, look over the draft, approve it.
- [ ] After approving, Pomona asks once about notifications. Say yes.
- [ ] Shop shows the list. Tick a few things off.
- [ ] Open tonight's dinner on Cook and go through a couple of steps.
- [ ] Ask Pomona for a change ("Can we swap Friday for something quicker?")
      and see the plan change.

## The phone's own features

- [ ] Notifications: a notification from Pomona shows on the lock screen.
      (Only once the push key is in Railway. Skip this if it isn't yet.)
- [ ] Camera: take a photo of a handwritten list on Shop, or of a cookbook
      page when adding a recipe. Pomona asks for the camera first, then
      reads the photo.
- [ ] Dictation: in Ask, tap the microphone and say something. The words
      show up in the box.
- [ ] Invite: Preferences → Who's here → invite someone. The phone's share
      sheet opens with a message ready to send. (You don't have to send it.)

## No signal

- [ ] Open Shop with signal, so the list loads.
- [ ] Turn on Airplane mode. Swipe Pomona away and open it again. The list
      still opens.
- [ ] Turn Airplane mode off.

## Leaving

- [ ] Preferences → Delete your household → type DELETE → Delete. You see
      the goodbye screen.
- [ ] "Back to the start" on the goodbye screen takes you to sign in.
- [ ] Sign up again with the same email. It works, as a brand-new household.
      Then delete that one too.

## When you're done

Send Claude what didn't tick, with your screenshots. Once both phones are
clean, it's on to step 11 of `docs/TESTFLIGHT_WALKTHROUGH.md`.
